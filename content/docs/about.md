**:site-name is a free, public modded Minecraft network, run by two people since 2019.**
Our public servers are free for everyone and nothing is pay to win, we just run the servers we wanted to play on.

::row
  :::card
  ## Who we are

  Hi! We're **dukcc** and **Oz**.

  It started in 2019, when dukcc put up a community server so Oz and his viewers
  had somewhere to play while he was streaming. That was supposed to be the whole
  thing.

  People kept playing though, so we kept it running, maintaining it little by
  little for years.

  Then in 2025 we started going a tad overboard: we bought our own hardware, moved into a
  colocation, and incorporated the network (for funny tax reasons).
  :::

  :::card
  ## What we run

  Modded Minecraft servers! :br
  Which packs exactly changes over time, so have a look at the servers in the sidebar for what's up right now. :br

  :br

  All of them are open to everyone. No whitelist, no application, no donation
  needed. Just pick a pack and join at :server-host{code short-name="<pack>"} !

  The only exception are brand-new packs, which sometimes run as a
  [pre-release](/docs/donations/pre-release) for a while before we open them up to
  everyone.
  :::
::

## What we promise you

*Some things aren't going to change, no matter how big this gets.*

::row
  :::card
  ## No pay to win

  We're strictly against pay to win, and that's never going to change. :br

  :br

  Donating will get you bragging rights, some chat and nickname formatting and early
  access to [pre-release](/docs/donations/pre-release) servers, that's it. The
  [donations](/docs/donations) page has the details.

  Everything that actually does something in-game (chunk claims, extra homes,
  `/fly`, even `/god`!) is earned by playtime across the network. Check out the
  [ranks](/docs/ranks) page for what you get and when.
  :::

  :::card
  ## An awesome community

  Our staff team is honestly great, and so are the people playing here! :br
  Ask something in chat or in our Discord and someone will usually help you out within minutes.

  :br

  The best example is `/warp public`, a free for all area on our servers where everyone can build. :br
  Players set up shops there, dump their spare items for whoever needs them, and work on builds together. :br
  Have a look around next time you're online or check out [The Gallery](/gallery)
  :::

  :::card
  ## We don't kill servers

  We never shut down an active server, and we never reset maps. :br

  :br

  The few servers we did shut down over the years only went after a long time with nobody playing on them. :br
  And even those aren't gone: the maps stay downloadable in [The Graveyard](/graveyard). :br
  So if you come back months later, you can still grab your world and keep playing locally.
  :::

  :::card
  ## Powerful hardware

  We rent space, not servers! :br
  All of them are built by us, with parts we picked specifically for modded Minecraft. :br

  :br

  Modded packs mostly want fast single core performance, so that's what we buy:

  - modern Ryzen CPUs
  - as much RAM as we can fit on the board
  - and NVMe RAIDs. :br
    And all of it is ours so we're not at the mercy of hosting providers not upcharging the RAM we already have c:
  :::
::
